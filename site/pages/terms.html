What you share and allow

Zubi serves the reminders, calendars and notes you choose to every device you pair with it, and lets them add and edit items wherever you chose Read & Write. You decide what to share, what may be changed, and which devices may connect. Changes made through Zubi are real changes to your data and sync like any other; Zubi never deletes anything, but it can overwrite a title, note or date where you allowed writing. Security of your setup

A pairing code lets whoever holds it reach your Mac. Keep each one as secret as a password, give every device its own, and give a client a new code, or remove it, under Settings › Clients if you think its code has been exposed, as the documentation describes.
