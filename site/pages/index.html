Your Mac’s reminders, calendars and notes, on everything else.

A free Mac app that shares the reminders, calendars and notes you choose with your other devices: a Raspberry Pi, a Linux laptop, an AI assistant. From anywhere, encrypted. Install it, paste a code, done.

Three steps

  1. Install Zubi on your Mac

    Download it, move it to Applications and open it. It connects on its own and starts with your Mac from now on.

  2. Choose what to share

    In Zubi’s Settings, pick the lists, calendars and folders your devices may see. Everything else stays private.

  3. Pair a device

    Install the client, run it, and paste the code from Zubi’s menu (Copy Pairing Code).

    curl -fsSL https://zubi.fa-krug.de/install.sh | sh
zubi

Then use it

  • On a display

    Today’s events and the shopping list on a Raspberry Pi by the door, updated when something changes.

    zubi events
zubi watch

  • With an AI assistant

    Let Claude or any MCP client read your schedule and tick off reminders.

    zubi mcp

Private by default

Only what you choose

Nothing is shared until you pick it, and Zubi never deletes anything.

Encrypted end to end

Only your Mac and your devices can read what passes between them.

No account

Nothing to sign up for, and no tracking.
